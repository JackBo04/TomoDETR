# Scientific visual assets

The interactive viewer uses the three crops and recorded prediction coordinates from the current manuscript’s qualitative comparison figure. Backgrounds are mean projections (15.0, 59.3, and 26.0 nm slabs); all prediction matches were computed in 3D. Coordinates are projected into the same XY frame for display. Image intensities, crop bounds, and matching labels follow the figure’s recorded arrays.

Cases: CZII TS_89_8 apo-ferritin; DeePiCt VPP TS_0001 ribosome; CZII TS_15_8 beta-galactosidase. Methods: DeepETPicker, DeepFinder, OCTOPI, TomoDETR without CWQR, and TomoDETR. These are selected qualitative examples; aggregate results are shown separately.

The web animation moves only the comparison divider; it does not interpolate, synthesize, or alter predictions. Displayed counts refer to the full crop.

Tomographic data remain subject to their original dataset terms. The code’s MIT license does not relicense underlying CZII or DeePiCt data. This project redisplays the scientific figure excerpts with their dataset and method attribution.
